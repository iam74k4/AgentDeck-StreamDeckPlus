Push to Talk

What you say becomes the preset's input, so the preset decides the framing and where it goes — the active session, a new one, or the clipboard.

Privacy

Recognition is done by Windows' own speech recogniser, on this machine. No audio and no transcript is sent anywhere by AgentDeck, and nothing is written to disk or to the log. Only the recognised text reaches the agent you send it to.

The key records only while it is held, and the deck shows LISTENING the whole time. Releasing the key stops the microphone.
